Introduction

Emerging technologies are reshaping project management by offering innovative ways to enhance efficiency, collaboration, and decision-making. This transformation ultimately impacts customer satisfaction, which is a critical factor in project success.

Objectives

  • To identify key emerging technologies in project management.
  • To evaluate the impact of these technologies on customer satisfaction.
  • To analyze challenges and considerations in adopting these technologies.
  • To present a case study on the implementation of these technologies in a real-world scenario.

Literature Review

Emerging technologies such as AI, ML, blockchain, IoT, VR/AR, advanced data analytics, cloud-based tools, and RPA are transforming project management practices. These technologies offer new ways to enhance efficiency, collaboration, and decision-making, ultimately impacting customer satisfaction.

Methodology

This study involves data collection through surveys and interviews with project managers and clients, followed by statistical analysis to evaluate the impact of technologies on customer satisfaction. Additionally, a case study on Zenith Bank's implementation of emerging technologies is analyzed.

Impact of Technology on Project Management

Enhanced collaboration, improved decision-making, and efficient resource management are some of the key benefits of using emerging technologies in project management.

Customer Satisfaction

Emerging technologies help project managers understand and meet customer expectations more effectively, enhance communication, and ensure transparency and accountability.

Challenges and Considerations

Adoption and integration, security and privacy, and cost and ROI are some of the challenges that organizations face when implementing new technologies.

Case Study: Zenith Bank

A study on Zenith Bank in Lagos State found that technological changes significantly affect customer satisfaction. The study recommended adopting technology, training, participative leadership styles, effective communication, and cost minimization.

Conclusion

Emerging technologies have the potential to significantly enhance customer satisfaction in project management practices. By leveraging these technologies, project managers can improve collaboration, decision-making, and resource management, ultimately leading to better project outcomes and higher customer satisfaction.
